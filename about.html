About

This site is run by a learning and development enthusiast with a simple belief: knowledge is most useful when it is shared.

Why this site exists

AI is changing marketing faster than most people can follow. New tools, new methods and new risks appear every week. This site collects what matters, explains it in plain language, and points you to the original sources.

Who it is for

Anyone curious about what AI means for marketing: students, professionals, business owners and lifelong learners.

The approach

Gather knowledge. Understand it. Share it clearly. Always credit the source.

Keller's Brand Equity Pyramid

Kevin Lane Keller's model shows how a strong brand is built step by step, from being known to being loved. Each level rests on the one below it. Click any block to explore it.

← Rational route (head) Emotional route (heart) →
  • 1. Identity: Who are you?
  • 2. Meaning: What are you?
  • 3. Response: What do I think and feel about you?
  • 4. Relationship: What about you and me?

Example brand: Tony's Chocolonely, the Dutch chocolate brand built around a mission to end exploitation in the cocoa chain.

Source: Keller, K. L. (2001). Building customer-based brand equity. Marketing Management, 10(2), 14–19.

How we work

1. AI gathers

Each week, AI tools scan trusted marketing and technology sources for new developments.

2. AI summarises

Relevant items are summarised in plain language, focusing on what they mean for marketers.

3. A human checks

Every summary is reviewed before publishing. AI can make mistakes, so nothing goes live unchecked.

4. Sources are always linked

Each update links to its original source. We summarise; we do not copy. Read the original for full details.

Spotted an error?

If something is wrong or outdated, let us know and we will correct it.
